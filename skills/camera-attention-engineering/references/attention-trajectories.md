# Attention Trajectories

Attention can move without the observer moving.

## Handoff

One object points to the next:

`鞋印到墙根断了。墙上却多了一道湿手印。`

The first clue hands attention to the wall.

## Reverse

The observed thing becomes an actor toward the observer:

`他盯着窗外那只鸟。鸟慢慢把头转了过来。`

The camera does not move; the relation does.

## Failed handoff

A paragraph that jumps from table -> cloud -> childhood -> shoe with no causal, spatial, or associative bridge may be intentionally montage-like. If not, the reader pays reorientation cost without gaining structure.
