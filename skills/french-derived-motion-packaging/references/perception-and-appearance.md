# Perception et apparition

Sometimes the event is not movement through space but a change in **availability to perception**.

Useful French resources can include:

- `apercevoir` — catch sight of / perceive;
- `entrevoir` — glimpse, partly perceive;
- `surgir` — appear suddenly/emerge, with strong eventive force;
- `se profiler` — become outlined / stand out in view.

## Test

Before choosing a motion verb, ask:

1. Did the object move?
2. Did the observer move?
3. Did an obstruction change?
4. Or did only recognition change?

Choose a predicate that commits to the right geometry.

## Transfer

Every language can distinguish **object motion**, **observer motion**, and **appearance** even if it lexicalizes them differently. The decomposition itself is the reusable technique.
