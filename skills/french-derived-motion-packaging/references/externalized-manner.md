# Manière externalisée

Manner can live outside the main verb through a gerund, adverbial phrase, subordinate clause, or descriptive addition.

Example:

`Elle descendit l'escalier en courant.`

The event spine is descent; running modifies how it occurs.

## Why externalize

- keep path foregrounded;
- background manner;
- avoid a main verb that overstates attitude;
- preserve clause hierarchy.

## Failure

Do not externalize manner merely because a typology chart says to. If the manner is the point, a manner-rich lexical choice may be more exact.

## Transfer

Externalizing manner is a general revision tool. English can unpack a packed phrasal verb; Chinese can move manner to an adverbial or serial action; Japanese can distribute it through mimetic or subordinate material.
