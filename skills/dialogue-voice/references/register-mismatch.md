# Register Mismatch

Common leak sources:

- therapy: `边界 / 创伤 / 情绪价值 / 关系模式`
- management: `复盘 / 阶段 / 目标 / 长期发展 / 策略`
- criticism: `象征 / 叙事 / 意义 / 结构`
- sociology: `身份 / 权力关系 / 社会规训`

None is forbidden. The question is whether the speaker owns the register.

## Repair without dumbing down

Replace abstract packaging with the speaker's **working objects**.

A cook may express timing through heat and texture.
A bus driver may express delay through stops and lights.
A child may express fairness through turns, debts, promises, and possession.

The reasoning can remain exact while the vocabulary becomes local.
