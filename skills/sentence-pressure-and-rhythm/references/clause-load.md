# Clause Load

A long sentence can carry many clauses if they share one path.

## Join when

- actions form one uninterrupted trajectory;
- attention moves through a continuous field;
- subordinate facts all constrain one delayed resolution.

## Split when

- the grammatical subject changes and the reader must rebuild the scene;
- one clause begins explanation after the event has already landed;
- a new time scale or causal graph begins;
- the sentence changes from observation to essay without purpose.

## Quick test

Underline the main verb path. If you need two unrelated underlines, consider a boundary.
