# Repetition and Release

Repetition earns space when the repeated form changes its value.

## Productive recurrence

`他敲了一次。`
`又敲。`
`第三次，门里也响了一下。`

The repeated action accumulates expectation; the third occurrence changes the source of sound.

## Empty recurrence

`他很冷。手也冷。空气也冷。房间也很冷。`

Unless the widening scale is intentional, the repetition merely restates temperature.

## Test

After each recurrence, ask: **what can the reader now predict that they could not predict before?**
