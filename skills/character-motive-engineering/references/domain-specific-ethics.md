# Domain-Specific Ethics

Concern can be partitioned.

A character may apply one rule to family, another to strangers, another to property, another to animals, institutions, debts, competitors, or customers. The partition can be coherent without being morally admirable.

## Useful distinction

**Cognitive accuracy** is not the same as **moral inclusion**.

A person may model another creature or person very accurately because prediction is useful, while granting that target little moral weight. Conversely, affection can be intense while observation is poor.

## Write the boundary, not a diagnosis

Weak: `He lacks empathy.`

Better generator question: `Which entities count as someone he owes? Which count as problems, assets, witnesses, family, or scenery?`

The domain map should predict behavior before the narrator judges it.
