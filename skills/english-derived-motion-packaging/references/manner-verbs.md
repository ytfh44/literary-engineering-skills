# Manner Verbs

A manner verb earns its specificity when the manner changes prediction.

## Useful questions

- Does gait reveal injury, haste, stealth, weight, surface, or control?
- Will the manner affect sound, timing, balance, or what happens next?
- Is the manner observed from this viewpoint?

## Minimal pair

`She crossed the kitchen.`

`She tiptoed across the kitchen.`

The second adds stealth/care and changes expected sound. If nobody is being avoided and sound does not matter, `tiptoed` may be gratuitous characterization.

## Rarity trap

`perambulate`, `saunter`, `stride`, `stalk` are not upgrades. Each adds a different event commitment. Choose by event, not prestige.

## Transfer

Even where the target language prefers a general motion verb, the decomposition remains useful: ask whether manner deserves its own adverbial, participial, serial, or mimetic carrier.
