# Entering the Field of View

English readily lets a stationary object appear to move because the observer moves:

`The ridge swung into view.`
`A church tower rose above the roofs.`
`The road opened onto the sea.`

This can compress **observer motion + occlusion change + reveal**.

## Test

Recover the literal geometry:

1. What actually moves?
2. What obstruction or angle changes?
3. Why does the object become visible now?

If there is no recoverable geometry, the apparent-motion verb may be decorative.

## Transfer

The underlying operation is “make visibility change an event.” Other languages can realize it with a reveal verb, observer motion, direction/result construction, or clause order without copying English apparent motion.
