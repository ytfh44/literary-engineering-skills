# Satellites and Path

English frequently places path outside the main verb:

`run out / crawl under / drift past / push through / glance up`

This permits a compact division of labor:

`verb = manner/action`
`particle/preposition = path/orientation`

## Audit

- Is the particle spatial, aspectual, idiomatic, or several at once?
- Does adding a second satellite clarify path or merely stack direction words?
- Can a single lexical verb (`enter`, `leave`, `cross`) say the event more cleanly?

## Counterexample

`He exited out of the room` duplicates path. Packaging density is not license to repeat it.

## Transfer

Use the decomposition on French, Chinese, Japanese, or any other target even if final packaging reverses the allocation: path may move into the verb and manner outside it.
