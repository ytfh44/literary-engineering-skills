# Label-Breaking Sequences

Order scenes so the reader repeatedly fits a common label, then discovers the hidden variable.

## Sequence pattern

1. **Sample 1** suggests label A.
2. **Sample 2** strengthens A.
3. **Sample 3** violates A but preserves generator G.
4. Reader revises to label B.
5. **Sample 4** violates B and again preserves G.
6. Later, the reader predicts G directly.

## Example

G: `He treats promises as property transfers.`

- keeps a silly promise -> “loyal”
- refuses to promise comfort -> “cold”
- breaks a friendship rather than make a promise he cannot keep -> neither label suffices

The sequence is successful when the reader eventually thinks “of course he did that” before finding an adjective.
