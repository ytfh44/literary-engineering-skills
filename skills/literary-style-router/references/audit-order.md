# Audit Order

Repair low-level causes before high-level decoration.

Default order when several mechanisms are genuinely active:

1. **Event integrity** — who does what, to whom, with what result?
   (`verb-event-engineering`, `predicate-licensing-and-personification`)
2. **Viewpoint and access** — from where and in what order can the reader
   know it? (`camera-attention-engineering`,
   `adverb-particle-viewpoint-engineering`)
3. **Knowledge** — who knows what when, and does any character act on a
   boundary? (`knowledge-boundaries`)
4. **Evidence** — what concrete event supports the state or inference?
   (`narrator-intervention-abstraction-control`)
5. **Voice** — who owns the wording? (`dialogue-voice`)
6. **Relation language** — which explanations are now redundant?
   (`relation-scaffolding`)
7. **Naming and property** — what do the modifiers and names activate?
   (`property-and-adjective-engineering`, `naming-and-address-engineering`)
8. **Metaphor** — does an image add object-specific information?
   (`metaphor-audit`)
9. **Pressure and rhythm** — does sentence timing match information
   release? (`sentence-pressure`)

Why this order: a metaphor cannot rescue a misbuilt event; a short sentence
cannot rescue a misplaced observer. Later passes should not force earlier
structure to move again.

**State check:** before executing more than one or two passes, consult
`literary-strategy-controller` and record what the document already does
heavily. Six correct local fixes in a row are one house style.

Break the order only when the user asks for a single narrow operation.
