# Valency and Roles

A verb licenses a participant pattern. Literary revision often fails because it swaps synonyms while leaving this pattern untouched.

## Useful roles

- **Agent**: acts with control or intention.
- **Cause**: brings about change without necessarily intending it.
- **Patient**: undergoes change.
- **Experiencer**: perceives or feels.
- **Instrument**: mediates action.
- **Path/Goal/Source**: structures motion.
- **Result**: the state created by the event.

These are diagnostics, not labels every sentence must expose.

## Example

`风吹开门` makes wind a cause and door a patient with a result.

`门开了` suppresses the cause.

`他把门推开` introduces an agent and controlled action.

The physical endpoint may match. The event does not.
