# Responsibility and Agency

Verb choice can assign moral weight before the narrator comments.

Compare event framings:

- `马杀犬` — killer/victim frame.
- `马践死犬` — physical mechanism plus result.
- `犬死奔马之下` — victim-centered outcome.

No version is “more vivid” in the abstract. Each places responsibility and attention differently.

## Audit questions

- Does the verb imply intention that the facts do not support?
- Does it erase an agent the story needs to judge?
- Does it convert accident into action, or action into accident?
- Does a passive or result construction move sympathy without changing facts?

Use the weakest commitment that remains true unless stronger commitment is part of the narrative judgment.
