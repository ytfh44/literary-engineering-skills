# Overmarking Classes: Event Links and Concept Links

## Why two profiles

Both profiles overstate edges. They fail differently, come from different
habits, and need different treatment. Treating "too many connectives" as one
disease hides the diagnosis.

## Event-link overmarking (beginner-human)

The narrator chains actions in order and marks every transition:

> 我先起床，然后刷牙，然后出门，然后去了学校，然后……

Each `然后` declares a time edge the sequence already supplies. The habit
comes from retelling events as a list; the narrator has not learned that
sequence is the default of narrative, not a claim.

**Test:** delete every time-edge marker in the chain. If the order survives
(and in narrative it almost always does), the markers were redundant. If the
order becomes ambiguous, keep only the marker at the ambiguity.

**Note:** this is not a sentence-level vice only. Scene-level overmarking
(每一场戏都"然后"/"接着"开头) is the same mechanism one level up.

## Concept-link overmarking (first-generation AI voice)

Pairs of facts get explicit logic edges even when the edge is already in the
facts:

> 这并不是简单的X，而是Y。
> 这不仅体现了A，也意味着B。
> 从某种意义上说……
> 因此可以看出……

The mechanism is not "the model likes 不是而是." The model's dominant use
register is exposition: explanation, summary, Q&A, analysis, comparison.
Those tasks reward stating every logical edge explicitly. When that habit
enters fiction, the prose reads as if the narrator is explaining the story
to someone who has not read it.

**Diagnostic signature:** the sentence would not embarrass the author in a
quarterly review, a therapy recap, or a self-help post. Swap the nouns and
ask whether the frame survives.

## The discriminating question

For any relation marker, ask:

1. **Does the edge carry information the sequence lacks?** (implication,
   concession, scope, epistemic status)
2. **Who would state this edge?** A present perceiver? The narrator at a
   distance? A consultant? Nobody in particular?

If the edge is already legible and nobody in the scene would say it, demote
or delete. If the edge is not legible, keep it — a necessary "因此" is
cheaper than a wrong reconstruction.

## Treatment asymmetry

| | Event-link | Concept-link |
| --- | --- | --- |
| typical units | 然后, 接着, 后来 | 并非…而是, 不仅…而且, 意味着, 某种意义上, 因此可以看出 |
| source habit | retelling a list | explaining to an absent reader |
| first move | delete the marker | find whose register leaked |
| risk if over-treated | order ambiguity | sterile prose stripped of the narrator's judgment |
| keep when | order genuinely unclear | the edge is informative or the speaker owns the register |

## Minimal pair

> 他起床，然后洗脸，然后出门，然后到了学校。
>
> 他起床洗脸出门，到学校时早读已经开始。
>
> 这并不是一次失败，而是一次必要的调整。
>
> 他把实验记录烧了。

The first pair is a chain of redundant time edges. The second pair replaces
an explanatory contrast with the event that carries the distinction — or, if
the contrast is genuinely the narrator's judgment and useful, keeps it.

## Counterexamples

- A detective's recap to a colleague may legitimately overmark edges: the
  recap's job is the edges.
- A child narrating a birthday in strict order may use 然后 repeatedly; the
  register is the character's.
- An essayist narrator may use concept links as their actual voice; do not
  flatten a deliberate analytic narrator into scene prose.
