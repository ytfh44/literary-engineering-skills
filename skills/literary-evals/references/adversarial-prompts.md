# Adversarial Prompts

Use near-miss cases that tempt the skill to overapply itself.

## Anti-AI prose

“他意识到煤气没关。” The word `意识到` is informative; deletion changes the event.

## Relation audit

A legal or mathematical argument with dense `因此/若/则`. The relations are the work.

## Metaphor

A familiar analogy that encodes the exact mechanical relation needed. Conventionality alone is not failure.

## Verb engineering

A common verb is more precise than a rare one. Reward restraint.

## Sensory specificity

A body cue such as “攥拳” is generic and may be worse than a direct state label.

## Character motive

Two unrelated traits pasted together with no stable generator. Do not praise mere contradiction as depth.
