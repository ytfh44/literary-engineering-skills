# Information Pressure

Pressure rises when the reader must hold an unresolved variable while new constraints arrive.

## Mark four things

- **question opened**
- **constraint added**
- **prediction revised**
- **question released**

Example:

`门没锁。灯却关着。桌上的饭还是热的。`

Each sentence narrows the situation without resolving who is present. The rhythm comes from accumulating incompatible clues, not from shortness.

## Flat pressure

`门没锁，而且灯关着，同时桌上的饭还是热的。`

This may be logically equivalent, but relation scaffolding can flatten the successive updates into one report-like bundle.
