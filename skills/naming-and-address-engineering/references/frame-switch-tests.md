# Frame-Switch Tests for Naming

## The battery

For each naming act, run the frame test:

1. **Knowledge test.** What does the speaker know about the referent that
   justifies this name? 嫌疑人 declares an epistemic claim the speaker may
   not be entitled to; if the speaker knows no accusation, the name is a
   leak from the author.
2. **Relation test.** Which relation does the name activate — kinship,
   institution, neighborhood, hostility? Replace with the closest legal
   alternative and read the relation delta.
3. **Relevance test.** For the current decision or perception, is this
   identity the operative one? Calling the doctor 姐夫 while deciding
   whether to trust the diagnosis activates the wrong frame unless the
   scene is precisely about that collision.
4. **Distance test.** Does the name place the speaker nearer or farther than
   the scene's emotion? 父亲 vs 那个人 is a thermometer.
5. **Alignment test.** Whose perception does the narration adopt with this
   name? If the viewpoint character would not use it, the narrator has
   borrowed a frame.

## Continuity checks

- An unmarked name switch is a signal. Decide: the frame changed (keep, and
  the reader should feel it) or the frame did not change (repair the name).
- A character who never renames is carrying a fixed relation; a character
  who renames freely is showing instability or role performance. Both are
  facts, not errors.
- Watch the narrator's default name: it is the work's baseline relation to
  the character. Every departure from it is loaded.

## Minimal pair

> 李叔把三轮车停在巷口。
>
> 老李把三轮车停在巷口。

If the narrator has always called him 李叔 and this is the first 老李, the
shift ages the relationship. If the narrator is a newcomer who learned the
nickname, the shift is knowledge, not distance. The test is who changed.

> 那个男人走过来。
>
> 父亲走过来。

Same walk, opposite worlds. If the scene is the son's and the estrangement
is the point, the first name is the event. If the scene needs the kinship
frame, the second is exact. Variety is not a reason to switch either way.

## Counterexamples

- Institutional role performance: 主任 in the office, 老李 at the funeral.
  The name is context-shaped, not deceitful.
- A child learning to call a step-parent 爸爸: the naming act is the plot.
  Do not normalize it into one consistent name.
- Mistaken identity names (calling the suspect 病人 until the reveal) are
  information management; the reveal is partly a rename.
