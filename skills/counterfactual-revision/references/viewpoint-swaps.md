# Viewpoint Swaps

Do not rewrite the whole scene to test viewpoint.

Possible single-variable swaps:

- 来 <-> 去
- “我看见 X” <-> “X 进入视野”
- observer moves before reveal <-> reveal occurs before observer move
- active <-> passive when event facts stay fixed
- direct perception <-> inferred perception

For each, ask: **where does the reader now stand, and when do they gain access?**

A good swap changes perceptual geometry without silently changing plot facts.
