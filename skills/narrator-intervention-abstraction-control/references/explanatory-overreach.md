# Explanatory Overreach

An explanatory sentence overreaches when it closes an inference that the scene has already made available and the closure adds no new fact.

## Deletion test

Delete the explanatory sentence. Ask three questions:

1. What factual information vanished?
2. What inference became impossible rather than merely less explicit?
3. Did the local viewpoint become less intelligible?

If the answers are “none, none, no,” the sentence was probably commentary after the event.

## Typical after-sentence

> 他把门锁了两遍。显然，他仍然无法摆脱内心的不安。

The second sentence names the reading most readers already have. It earns its place only if the narrator's judgment itself matters.

## Counterexample

> 他把门锁了两遍，不是怕贼，是怕父亲回来。

The explanation changes the causal model. Keep it.
