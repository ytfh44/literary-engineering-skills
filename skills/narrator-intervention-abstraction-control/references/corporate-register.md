# Corporate Register in Fiction

The problem is not vocabulary origin. It is **institutional abstraction without an institution in the scene**.

## Common shapes

- phase/status language: 阶段、状态、进展、长期、方向
- review language: 重新审视、复盘、反思、调整策略
- relation management: 边界、关系模式、互动方式、沟通机制
- abstract outcomes: 成长、改变、认知迭代、达成共识

A word becomes suspicious when it names a dashboard variable instead of an experience the current voice would actually track.

## Test

Replace the characters with a team and a project. If the sentence survives with almost no syntactic change, ask whether the fiction has outsourced perception to management prose.

### Minimal pairs

- “他们需要重新审视彼此的关系。” -> “他把她的杯子也洗了，没再问她什么时候走。”
- “她完成了一次重要的自我调整。” -> “她删掉那封信，又把垃圾箱清空。”

Do not prefer the right side mechanically. Prefer it when the event is the information.

## Keep it when

- the speaker genuinely uses managerial language;
- the narrator is deliberately essayistic;
- the abstraction compresses a long timespan that the story does not need to dramatize;
- the sentence introduces a new proposition rather than paraphrasing visible action.
